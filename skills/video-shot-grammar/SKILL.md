# Shot grammar and cinematic direction

**Trigger:** Use for tasks involving plan coherent shot sequences that communicate intent through spatial continuity, framing and motivated camera movement.

## Operational procedure

1. Write a one-sentence viewer outcome and assign each shot a single narrative role: establish, reveal, react, transition or resolve.
2. Specify shot size, lens perspective, camera position/height, movement and subject blocking; make motion motivated rather than decorative.
3. Explicitly establish screen direction, 180-degree axis, eyelines, character scale and transitions across adjacent shots.
4. Choose shot length by speaking rate, visual complexity and editing rhythm; use frame counts at project FPS, not arbitrary floating timestamps.
5. Provide a shot list with acceptance frames and distinguish what must be filmed/generated from what code compositing can supply.
6. For each shot document its cheapest fallback if required camera/model capability is missing.

**Check:** Evidence supports the outcome, protected constraints remain satisfied, limitations are explicit and executable checks were actually run. For unavailable capabilities, mark the step NOT EXECUTED.
