# Editorial storytelling and attention

**Trigger:** Use for tasks involving iterate video rhythm through perceptible viewer outcomes rather than adding arbitrary effects.

**Purpose:** Improve outcomes by applying an explicit, testable operational method rather than relying on generic instructions.

## Operational procedure

1. Write a hook, setup, escalation and payoff with target viewer and intended takeaway.
2. Map every second to information or emotion; remove redundant shots before increasing VFX complexity.
3. Test silent viewing and audio-only understanding for content that must work on mobile.
4. Compare at least two edits for clarity, pacing and memorability with viewers or controlled rubric.
5. Check that humor, transitions and music support the actual story, not generic templates.
6. Track negative viewer feedback and revise only demonstrably weak sections.

**Check:** Evidence supports the outcome, protected constraints remain satisfied, limitations are explicit and executable checks were actually run. For unavailable capabilities, mark the step NOT EXECUTED.
